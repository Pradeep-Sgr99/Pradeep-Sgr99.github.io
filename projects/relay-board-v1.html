RELAY Board V1.0

PoE-powered 10-channel relay controller with 2 PWM outputs, wired Ethernet and BLE control
Role: Hardware design & PCB layout Company: OSEC MCU: STM32F401CC (Cortex-M4F) Wireless: ESP32 (BLE) Power: IEEE 802.3 PoE PD / DC input

Overview

RELAY Board V1.0 is a 10-channel relay controller plus two PWM-controlled power outputs (for LED brightness and other dimmable loads), running from Power-over-Ethernet with a DC power-jack / XT30 fallback input. An STM32F401 handles the relay and PWM logic and arbitrates between two control paths: a wired IP interface through a WIZnet W5500 hardwired TCP/IP controller, and a BLE interface via an ESP32 module. The PoE front end, isolated conversion and a 12 V / 5 V / 3.3 V buck chain are all on-board, so a single Ethernet cable delivers both data and power.

Power architecture

  • Source: IEEE 802.3 PoE on the RJ45 — PM8805 handles detection / classification, inrush-limited hot-swap and the primary-side switching of the isolated step-down. A DC power-jack and an XT30 input feed the same downstream chain for bench or non-PoE use.
  • Stage 1 — LM5164 (100 V buck): PoE bus → 12 V rail, with headroom for PoE bus transients.
  • Stage 2 — TPS564247 (6 A buck): 12 V → 5 V relay-coil / system rail.
  • Stage 3 — 3.3 V buck: 5 V → 3.3 V logic for the STM32, W5500 and ESP32.
  • Relay coils: 5 V, each switched low-side by a DMG3402L logic-level N-channel MOSFET driven straight from an MCU GPIO, with a freewheel diode across every coil.
  • PWM outputs: the two XT30 output channels are switched by power MOSFETs gated by the TC4427 from MCU timer PWM — used for LED brightness and other dimmable / variable loads.
  • Isolation: a barrier separates the PoE front-end / converter primary from the logic secondary.

Firmware & control

  • STM32F401 owns relay state and interlocks; both control paths issue requests and the MCU arbitrates (last-command / priority, with a safe default state on link loss).
  • Wired path: W5500 hardware sockets expose relay control over TCP — Modbus TCP / JSON / raw command set — with the IP stack fully offloaded from the MCU.
  • BLE path: ESP32 runs a GATT service for relay actuation, naming, grouping and live status, bridged to the STM32 over UART.
  • Supervision: rail monitoring, per-channel state feedback and a watchdog-backed fail-safe.

Layout notes

  • Two banks of five relays with the screw terminals on the board edges; each coil returned through its DMG3402L close to the relay, and the 5 V coil rail kept clear of the SPI / control routing.
  • The two PWM output MOSFETs and their XT30 terminals placed near the 12 V rail with short, wide high-current paths; TC4427 gate drive kept tight to the MOSFET gates.
  • PoE front end and the isolated converter grouped at the RJ45 end, behind the isolation barrier and away from the logic and the ESP32 antenna.
  • ESP32 module placed with its antenna over a board-edge keep-out; W5500 SPI kept short to the STM32.
  • Wide-Vin Stage-1 converter and bulk input filtering near the power entry; separate power and logic grounds joined at a single point.

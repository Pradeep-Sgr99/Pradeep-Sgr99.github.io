LH Power Supply Board V1.0

12 V-in battery-charging power supply — eFuse protection, buck-boost charger, regulated system rails
Role: Hardware design & PCB layout Product: LightScope (lighthearted) Input: 12 V DC only (no USB/PD) Battery: 2S2P Li-ion (4 cells) Stack-up: 4-layer Control: I²C (charger + monitor ADC)

Overview

The LH Power Supply Board takes a single 12 V DC input (no USB/PD) and turns it into a battery-backed, monitored supply for the LightScope system. A TPS25942 eFuse guards the input against over-voltage, over-current and inrush; a BQ25792 buck-boost charger runs a 2S2P (4-cell) Li-ion pack and holds up VSYS through its integrated power path; and a two-step regulator chain provides the +5 V and +3.3 V rails. An ADS7128 8-channel I²C ADC monitors the rail voltages and current senses so the host can see the full power state. Built on a 4-layer stack-up for the switching planes and return paths.

12 V DC inputeFuse protection2S2P Li-ion charger Integrated power path+5 V / +3.3 V rails8-ch I²C monitor ADC 4-layer

Power flow

12 V DC in → TPS25942 eFuse → BQ25792 charger + power path → VSYS → TPS564247 buck → +5 V → XC6220 LDO → +3.3 V

The BQ25792 also feeds and charges the 2S2P pack; on loss of the 12 V input it supplies VSYS from the battery through the same power path (no rail dropout).

Protection & monitoring

  • Input (TPS25942): current limiting, OV clamp and UVLO, controlled inrush and reverse-current blocking — downstream stages never see a fault or surge on the 12 V line.
  • Battery (BQ25792): 2S CC/CV profile with configurable current, battery thermistor (TS) sensing and safety timers; integrated ADC gives VBAT / IBAT / VBUS / IBUS without extra parts.
  • Rails (ADS7128): eight channels across the board's voltage dividers and current senses, read over I²C; the built-in comparators raise a flag on any rail excursion.
  • Charger and monitor ADC share one I²C bus to the host controller, with interrupt / power-good lines for fast fault response.

Layout notes

  • 4-layer stack-up: switching-node copper kept tight, a solid ground plane directly under the power stages, and separate analog ground for the monitor ADC and dividers.
  • eFuse and bulk input filtering at the connector; charger power inductor and the buck inductor placed with short high-current loops and their own local decoupling.
  • Battery, VSYS, +5 V and +3.3 V brought out on labelled pads / board-to-board headers to the rest of the LightScope stack.
  • Sense lines to the ADS7128 routed away from the switching nodes; Kelvin connections on the current-sense resistors.

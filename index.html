About Me

Hi! I'm Pradeepa N O, an Embedded Systems Engineer with 5+ years of experience across embedded hardware design and end-to-end PCB development.

I specialize in multilayer PCB design (4–6 layers), mixed-signal and high-speed layouts, power electronics (DC-DC, SMPS) and PoE-based power management. My work spans schematic design, component selection, firmware integration on STM32/ESP32, hardware validation, and manufacturing support including DFM/DFT and Gerber generation.
